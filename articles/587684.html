Best Settings for Smooth Gameplay in Cemu v2.1 on Ryzen 5 3600

By Aarontendo

Updated: 2024

8 min read OPTIMIZATION

If you're running Cemu v2.1 on a Ryzen 5 3600 and looking for optimal settings to achieve smooth gameplay, this guide will help you configure your emulator for the best performance. We'll focus on balancing visuals and speed, ensuring that you get the best experience possible with minimal stuttering and lag.

Graphics Settings

The following graphics settings are recommended to achieve smooth performance, especially when running demanding Wii U titles:

Setting Recommended Value
Graphics APIVulkan
Graphics DeviceYour native GPU
VSyncTriple Buffering
Upscale FilterBilinear
Downscale FilterBilinear
Fullscreen ScalingKeep aspect ratio

Why Vulkan over OpenGL?

Vulkan offers lower CPU overhead and better multi-threaded performance, which benefits the Ryzen 5 3600's 6 cores significantly. Most Wii U titles see measurable frame rate improvements with Vulkan on AMD hardware.

Why Triple Buffering?

Triple Buffering helps smooth out gameplay and menus. Unlike Double Buffering — which can cause slow gameplay even when menus run fine — Triple Buffering allows the GPU to work more efficiently by reducing the time spent waiting between frames. This results in a smoother overall experience without screen tearing or excessive input lag.

Shader Compilation

Shader compilation is essential for smooth gameplay, as it prevents stuttering when new graphical effects are rendered. Configure your shader settings as follows:

Setting Recommended Value
Asynchronous Shader CompilationEnabled
Precompiled Shader CacheEnabled

Enabling Asynchronous Shader Compilation allows shaders to compile in the background, reducing stuttering during gameplay. It's crucial for maintaining smooth performance in graphically intensive games. The Precompiled Shader Cache minimises initial stuttering when running a game for the first time.

VSync targets 60 fps — the human eye rarely benefits from higher frame rates in these titles. If you experience slowdown, closing background tasks is the quickest fix.

General Tips

  • Keep Your Drivers Updated: New AMD drivers often improve Vulkan compatibility and performance. Check Radeon Software regularly.
  • Close Background Applications: Free up system resources by closing unnecessary apps running in the background.
  • Use a High-Performance Power Plan: Set your power plan to High Performance in Windows to prevent CPU and GPU throttling.

Important Notes

  • Test Settings Per Game: While these settings provide a solid baseline, some games may require individual tweaks. Always test and adjust based on the specific title.
  • Graphic Mods: Some games have graphic mods available that can further improve frame rate or visual quality. Explore these to enhance your experience.
  • Tested Games: Settings were tested on New Super Mario Bros. U, New Super Luigi U, and Super Mario 3D World. Performance may vary with other titles.
  • Minor Graphical Glitches: Minor glitches may occur, such as backgrounds flickering when entering underground areas or vines loading a few frames late. These appear when first loading certain textures and do not impact gameplay.
  • Backup Your Configurations: Before making major changes, back up your Cemu settings so you can revert if necessary.
